The Hungering Dark

Beneath the old chapel, something has hatched. It is small, wet and hungry, and it has chosen you.

Your goal: raise it from a mewling Spawn into an Elder Horror.

  • Keep its Hunger, Devotion and Dread above zero. They drain slowly in real time.
  • Command cultists to perform short rites. Each cultist has a trait and a loyalty. Disloyal ones steal and flee. Preach a Sermon to keep them in line.
  • Tap the horror to stroke it. Hold to feed it an offering.
  • Answer Omens when they appear, and perform Rituals by repeating the rune sequence.
  • Rites earn Power. All Power you earn makes the horror grow; spend it in the Dark Works for upgrades. What it becomes depends on the rites and choices you favour.
  • If a need stays empty, the horror weakens and may die. Progress saves automatically, and time away can't kill it outright.

It is ended

The Ritual

The runes will glow in sequence. Watch closely, then touch them in the same order. Success floods the horror with Power; failure wounds it.
