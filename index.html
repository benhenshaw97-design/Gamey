The Hungering Dark

Beneath the old chapel, something has hatched. It is small, wet and hungry, and it has chosen you.

Your goal: raise it from a mewling Spawn into an Elder Horror.

  • It has three needs that slowly drain in real time: Hunger, Devotion and Dread. Keep them above zero.
  • Send your cultists to perform rites. Each rite takes a little while, then pays off. A cultist on a rite is busy until it ends.
  • Gather Power and the horror grows. What it becomes depends on which rites you favour.
  • If a need stays empty, the horror weakens, cultists flee, and it may die.
  • Progress saves automatically. Time keeps passing while you are away, but it won't kill your horror outright.

Tip: tap the horror. It likes being noticed.
