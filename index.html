How to Play

Beneath the old chapel, something has hatched. You lead the cult that feeds it.

  • Each day is one card. Swipe left or right, or tap a choice. The day only moves on when you decide.
  • Four meters: Hunger, Devotion, Dread and Suspicion. If any one empties or fills completely, the cult is finished. While you drag a card, glowing dots show which meters will move, but not by how much.
  • The Body. Some choices give you parts. Open The Body to graft them onto the grid. Parts must touch the flesh. Each part changes the daily drift of the meters, and some parts placed side by side create synergies. Moving or removing a part costs 1 Devotion.
  • Growth. The more mass you graft, the bigger it grows: Spawn, Larva, Abomination, Elder Horror. The grid grows with it, and new parts open up new choices on the cards.
  • The cult. Your cultists wander the ritual ground. Tap one to learn about them. Some choices cost a cultist, and a starving horror helps itself.
  • Every ending is remembered. Each death leaves an inheritance for your next cult. Can you find all ten endings?
